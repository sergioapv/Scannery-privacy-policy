# Privacy Policy **Scannery** **Last updated: September 29, 2026** Scannery ("the App") is a document and photo scanning application that allows users to digitize, enhance, organize, store, and share documents and photographs. This Privacy Policy explains how Scannery handles information when you use the App. We are committed to protecting your privacy and minimizing the collection of personal data. ## 1. Data Controller The data controller responsible for the processing of personal data in connection with Scannery is: **Developer:** Sérgio Valentim **Country:** Portugal **Email:** devsupport.address@gmail.com For any questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, you can contact us using the email address above. ## 2. Information We Access and Process ### Documents and Photographs Scannery allows you to scan physical documents and photographs using your device's camera or import existing images from your photo library. These may contain personal information, such as names, addresses, financial information, or other sensitive details, depending on the documents you choose to scan. Scannery uses this information to provide its core functionality, including: * Capturing documents and photographs. * Detecting document boundaries and correcting perspective. * Enhancing image quality, contrast, and readability. * Converting scans into PDF, JPEG, or PNG files. * Organizing scans into folders. * Allowing you to export and share your scans. Your scanned documents and photographs are processed and stored locally on your device. We do not receive, collect, or store the contents of your scans on our servers. ### Camera and Photo Library Scannery may request access to your device's camera to capture documents and photographs, and access to images you choose to import from your photo library. These permissions are used exclusively to provide the scanning and importing functionality you request. You can manage or revoke these permissions at any time through your device's settings, although doing so may limit certain features. ## 3. How Your Information Is Used Information accessed by Scannery is used to: * Provide document and photo scanning functionality. * Process, enhance, and convert images. * Store and organize documents within the App. * Allow you to export and share files. * Maintain the stability, security, and performance of the App, where applicable. We do not use the contents of your scanned documents for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes. ## 4. Data Collection and Analytics Scannery does not collect, track, or transmit personal data or technical usage information to the developer. The application does not use: * Analytics or user-tracking services. * Crash reporting or diagnostic tools. * Advertising SDKs. * Third-party services for collecting user data. Scannery does not collect device identifiers, usage statistics, or behavioral data. All document scanning, image processing, and storage are performed locally on your device. No scanned content or personal information is transmitted to the developer or to external servers by Scannery. ## 5. Sharing and Third-Party Services Scannery allows you to share or export documents using applications and services available on your device, such as email, messaging applications, and cloud storage providers. When you initiate a sharing action, the selected file is transferred to the destination you choose. That transfer is governed by the privacy policy and terms of the receiving service. Scannery does not automatically share your scanned documents or photographs with the developer or third parties. If third-party analytics, crash reporting, cloud storage, or other SDKs are integrated into the App, their relevant data processing practices and providers will be identified in this Privacy Policy. ## 6.
